The team's Car

Model : Mercedes-AMG F1 W17 E Performance

The W17 is the car Mercedes built for the 2026 season, the first one designed under the new technical regulations, with smaller dimensions, active aerodynamics and a new hybrid power unit.

Dimensions and Weight : 3.40 m wheelbase, 1.90 m wide, 768 kg

The 2026 cars are shorter, narrower and lighter than the previous generation: the wheelbase went down from 3.60 m to 3.40 m, the width from 2 m to 1.90 m, and the minimum weight, including the driver but without fuel, dropped by about 30 kg. These limits are the same for every team.

Engine : 1.6-litre V6 turbo hybrid

The power unit combines a combustion engine with an electric system that recovers energy under braking and stores it in a battery. Since 2026, the electric part provides about half of the total power, so energy management has become a key performance factor. Mercedes builds its own engine and also supplies McLaren, Williams and Alpine.

Front Wing : Corner mode / straight-line mode

The front wing is the first part of the car to meet the air. It generates downforce and directs the airflow towards the rest of the car. Since 2026, its flaps can move thanks to active aerodynamics, and every team designs its own.

Rear Wing : Active aerodynamics since 2026

The rear wing produces downforce at the back of the car. With active aerodynamics, it opens on the straights to reduce drag, which replaces the old DRS system.
