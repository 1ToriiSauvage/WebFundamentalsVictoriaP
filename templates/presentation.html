Team's Principal : Torger Christian Wolff (Aka : Toto)

Toto Wolff is an Austrian businessman and the CEO of the Mercedes Formula 1 team, in which he holds a one-third stake. Since joining Mercedes in 2013, he has led the team to eight consecutive Constructors' Championships between 2014 and 2021, making him one of the most successful team principals in the history of F1.

First Driver : George Russell (Aka : The Diva or Mister Saturday)

George Russell is a 28-year-old British driver who raced for Williams from 2019 to 2021 before joining Mercedes in 2022, the same year he secured his maiden victory in Brazil. Having become the team leader following Hamilton’s departure for Ferrari, he sat in second place in the 2026 championship standings as of mid-September, trailing his own teammate and a few points ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Second Driver : Andrea Kimi Antonelli (Aka : Kimi)

Kimi Antonelli is an Italian racing driver born on August 25, 2006, who replaced Lewis Hamilton at Mercedes in 2025 at the age of just 18. In 2026, he dominated the season: he won the driver's championship with four more races to go in Mexico with 125 points more than George Russell, a run that included a victory at Monza after starting from 19th place.

Reserv Driver : Frederik Vesti (Aka : Fred)

Frederik Vesti is a 24-year-old Danish racing driver who has been part of the Mercedes junior program since 2021 and finished as the Formula 2 runner-up in 2023. He serves as the sole reserve driver for Mercedes in 2026, ready to step in for Russell or Antonelli at a moment’s notice, while simultaneously competing in endurance racing in the United States (IMSA).
